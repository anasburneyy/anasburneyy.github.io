Get the apps

The newest build of both apps, always. Download yours on your phone, open it and tap Install. It goes over the one you have, so everyone's players and scores stay put.

The Burney SquadThe friends' app.
The Burney FamilyThe family's app.
Installing for the first time from your browser? Android asks first:
  1. Tap Settings on the message that pops up.
  2. Turn on Allow from this source.
  3. Go back and tap Install.
Already on a recent build? The app tells you about each new one on Home by itself. To look straight away, open Settings and tap Check now.
On an iPhone: there's no download. Open your app from the home page in Safari and add it to your Home Screen.
